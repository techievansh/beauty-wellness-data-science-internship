# Dataset Identification and Evaluation

## 1. Project Title
Customer Sentiment Analysis of Beauty Product Reviews

## 2. Objective
The objective is to identify and evaluate a publicly available dataset that can be used to analyze customer opinions, ratings, and satisfaction with beauty products.

## 3. Candidate Dataset
**Dataset Name:** Amazon Beauty Reviews

**Platform:** Hugging Face

**Dataset Link:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/jhan21/amazon-beauty-reviews-dataset

## 4. Required Data Fields
The following fields will be checked in the selected dataset:

- Review text: To understand customer opinions and complaints.
- Rating: To examine customer satisfaction.
- Product identifier: To group reviews by product.
- Review date: To study review patterns over time.
- Verified purchase indicator: To distinguish verified-purchase reviews, if available.

The actual column names and availability will be confirmed after inspecting the dataset.

## 5. Evaluation Criteria

**Relevance:** The dataset should contain reviews related to beauty products.

**Accessibility:** The data should be publicly accessible and downloadable.

**Completeness:** Review text and ratings should contain sufficient usable records.

**Quality:** Missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent ratings should be checked.

**Format:** The data should be available in a format that can be processed using Python.

**License:** The applicable license and usage restrictions must be reviewed.

## 6. Advantages
The dataset may provide a large collection of customer feedback. Review text and ratings can help identify satisfaction patterns, common complaints, and possible sentiment categories.

## 7. Limitations
The dataset may contain incomplete reviews, duplicate records, biased opinions, or imbalanced rating distributions. Large files may also require sampling before analysis.

## 8. Preliminary Evaluation
Amazon Beauty Reviews is the preferred candidate because it may provide diverse beauty-product feedback. However, the final selection will depend on actual file accessibility, column availability, data quality, and licensing.

## 9. Next Steps
The next steps are to inspect the dataset files, confirm the schema, examine a sample of records, and conduct initial data quality checks before beginning exploratory data analysis.

## 10. Conclusion
This evaluation establishes a systematic approach for selecting an appropriate dataset for customer sentiment analysis in the beauty and wellness industry.
