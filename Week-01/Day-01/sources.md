Provides insights into developments in the beauty industry, including consumer behavior, e-commerce, social commerce, personalization, and the role of technology.

Source:
https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-insights/state-of-beauty

## Research Areas Covered

The sources and research for Day 1 were used to understand:

* Beauty & Wellness industry structure
* Major beauty industry segments
* Consumer behavior
* E-commerce and digital commerce
* Personalization
* Social media and influencer marketing
* Customer reviews and ratings
* Artificial Intelligence applications
* Data Science opportunities in the industry
