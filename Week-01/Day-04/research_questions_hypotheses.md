# Research Questions and Hypotheses

## 1. Introduction

After selecting Customer Sentiment Analysis of Beauty Product Reviews as the business problem, the next step is to define research questions and hypotheses. These will guide the analysis and help identify meaningful patterns in customer feedback.

## 2. Research Questions

**RQ1:** What is the distribution of positive, negative, and neutral customer sentiment in the selected beauty product reviews?

**RQ2:** Which beauty products or product categories receive relatively higher or lower customer ratings?

**RQ3:** What product attributes, such as quality, fragrance, texture, price, packaging, and effectiveness, are frequently mentioned in customer reviews?

**RQ4:** What are the most common complaints expressed by customers in negative reviews?

**RQ5:** Is there an association between customer ratings and sentiment identified from review text?

**RQ6:** Do customer sentiment patterns differ across products or categories?

## 3. Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1: Ratings and Sentiment

**H0:** There is no association between customer ratings and sentiment classification derived from review text.

**H1:** Customer ratings are associated with sentiment classification derived from review text.

### Hypothesis 2: Differences Across Products

**H0:** Average customer ratings do not differ significantly across the selected products or categories.

**H1:** Average customer ratings differ significantly across at least some selected products or categories.

### Hypothesis 3: Negative Review Themes

**H0:** Negative reviews do not contain identifiable recurring product-related complaint themes beyond what would be expected from the overall distribution of review topics.

**H1:** Negative reviews contain recurring product-related complaint themes that can be identified through text analysis.

### Hypothesis 4: Review Length and Rating

**H0:** There is no statistically significant association between review length and customer rating.

**H1:** There is a statistically significant association between review length and customer rating.

## 4. How the Hypotheses Will Be Tested

- **Hypothesis 1:** Compare rating categories with text-based sentiment using a contingency table, chi-square test, and an appropriate association measure.
- **Hypothesis 2:** Compare ratings across groups using descriptive statistics and, where assumptions are met, ANOVA or a suitable non-parametric alternative.
- **Hypothesis 3:** Identify frequent words, phrases, and complaint themes in negative reviews using text analysis.
- **Hypothesis 4:** Calculate review length and examine its relationship with ratings using correlation or an appropriate alternative.

Statistical tests will be selected after checking sample size, variable types, independence, and relevant assumptions. Statistical significance alone will not be treated as proof of business importance.

## 5. Expected Outcomes

The analysis is expected to provide a clearer understanding of customer opinions, rating patterns, frequently mentioned product attributes, and recurring complaints. These findings may help businesses identify opportunities for product improvement and customer-focused marketing.

The hypotheses will be tested using the selected dataset. No outcome will be assumed before the analysis is performed.

## 6. Conclusion

The research questions and hypotheses provide a structured foundation for investigating customer sentiment in beauty product reviews. They connect the selected business problem with measurable variables and appropriate analytical methods.
