# Final Business Problem Selection — Beauty & Wellness

## 1. Introduction

The Beauty & Wellness industry generates customer feedback through online reviews, ratings, e-commerce platforms, and social media. This feedback can help businesses understand customer satisfaction, product performance, and changing preferences.

However, manually examining large volumes of reviews can be time-consuming. Data Science and Natural Language Processing (NLP) can help analyze this feedback systematically and identify useful business insights.

## 2. Selected Business Problem

**Customer Sentiment Analysis of Beauty Product Reviews to Identify Customer Preferences, Recurring Complaints, and Product Improvement Opportunities.**

## 3. Problem Statement

Beauty and personal care businesses receive large amounts of customer reviews containing opinions about product quality, effectiveness, fragrance, texture, price, packaging, and customer experience. Manually analyzing these reviews makes it difficult to identify recurring concerns and understand overall customer sentiment efficiently.

This project proposes a data-driven approach to analyze publicly available beauty product reviews, examine customer ratings, classify review sentiment, and identify frequently discussed product attributes. The resulting insights may help businesses understand customer feedback and make more informed product improvement and marketing decisions.

## 4. Objectives

1. Understand customer opinions about beauty products using publicly available review data.
2. Analyze the distribution of product ratings.
3. Identify positive, negative, and neutral sentiment where appropriate.
4. Discover common themes in customer reviews.
5. Examine differences in sentiment across products or categories, where the data permits.
6. Identify recurring complaints and positively mentioned product attributes.
7. Develop actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

## 5. Why This Problem Was Selected

The problem was selected because it has clear business relevance and can be explored using review text and rating data. It combines marketing analysis with Data Science techniques and provides opportunities for descriptive statistics, data visualization, text preprocessing, and NLP.

Public research datasets, including Amazon beauty reviews and Sephora product reviews, offer potential starting points. Their actual suitability, completeness, access conditions, and limitations will be evaluated during Week 2.

## 6. Expected Business Value

The analysis may help beauty businesses:

- Understand common customer likes and dislikes.
- Identify recurring product-related complaints.
- Compare customer feedback across products.
- Recognize product attributes associated with positive or negative opinions.
- Support product development and marketing decisions with evidence.

These are potential benefits, not guaranteed outcomes. Their usefulness will depend on data quality and the results of the analysis.

## 7. Preliminary Methodology

The proposed workflow is:

1. Identify and evaluate public beauty review datasets.
2. Select a suitable dataset and document its source.
3. Inspect data fields, missing values, duplicates, and coverage.
4. Clean review text and prepare rating variables.
5. Perform exploratory data analysis using statistics and visualizations.
6. Analyze sentiment using an appropriate rule-based or machine-learning method.
7. Examine recurring review themes and product-level patterns.
8. Summarize findings and provide evidence-based recommendations.

## 8. Scope and Limitations

The initial project will focus on publicly available beauty product reviews and associated ratings. It will not assume that reviews represent every customer or that sentiment alone proves a product's quality.

Potential limitations include biased reviewers, fake or duplicated reviews, missing data, outdated observations, language differences, and incorrect sentiment classification. These limitations will be considered during data evaluation and interpretation.

## 9. Conclusion

Customer Sentiment Analysis of Beauty Product Reviews has been selected as the proposed business problem because it connects customer feedback with practical business decisions and provides a feasible starting point for Data Science analysis.

The final dataset and methodology will be confirmed after the data sourcing and quality assessment in Week 2.
