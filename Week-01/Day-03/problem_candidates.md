# Beauty & Wellness Business Problem Candidates

## 1. Introduction

After studying the Beauty & Wellness industry and its current trends, several business problems were identified where Data Science can provide useful business insights.

The purpose of this stage is to compare potential problems and select one that is relevant to the Beauty & Wellness industry, feasible with available data, and suitable for further Data Science analysis.

---

## 2. Candidate Business Problems

### Candidate 1: Customer Sentiment Analysis

Beauty and wellness companies receive a large amount of customer feedback through online reviews and ratings. Manually analyzing this feedback can be difficult when the number of reviews becomes large.

Data Science and Natural Language Processing (NLP) can be used to analyze customer reviews, classify sentiment, identify common complaints, and understand customer preferences.

**Possible applications:**

* Positive, negative, and neutral sentiment classification
* Identification of common customer complaints
* Product-level sentiment analysis
* Analysis of customer satisfaction
* Identification of frequently mentioned product features

---

### Candidate 2: Customer Segmentation

Beauty and wellness businesses have customers with different preferences, purchasing behaviors, budgets, and product interests.

Customer segmentation can divide customers into meaningful groups using factors such as purchase frequency, spending, product preferences, and engagement.

**Possible applications:**

* Identifying high-value customers
* Grouping customers based on purchasing behavior
* Personalized marketing
* Targeted promotional campaigns
* Customer behavior analysis

---

### Candidate 3: Beauty Product Recommendation

Customers are often presented with a large number of beauty and wellness products. A recommendation system can help customers discover products that may match their interests and previous behavior.

Data Science can use customer-product interactions, ratings, reviews, product categories, and product characteristics to generate personalized recommendations.

**Possible applications:**

* Personalized product recommendations
* Similar product recommendations
* Product ranking
* Cross-selling and upselling
* Improved customer experience

---

### Candidate 4: Customer Purchase and Trend Prediction

Beauty and wellness preferences change over time because of seasonal trends, social media influence, new products, and changing customer preferences.

Historical customer and product data can be analyzed to identify patterns and potentially predict future customer trends.

**Possible applications:**

* Predicting customer preferences
* Identifying emerging product trends
* Seasonal customer behavior analysis
* Purchase prediction
* Trend identification

---

### Candidate 5: Sales and Demand Forecasting

Beauty and wellness businesses need to maintain sufficient inventory while avoiding overstocking.

Historical sales data can be used to identify demand patterns and forecast future product demand.

**Possible applications:**

* Product demand forecasting
* Inventory planning
* Seasonal sales analysis
* Identifying high-demand products
* Supporting business planning

---

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

The candidate problems will be evaluated using the following criteria:

1. **Business Relevance** – How useful the problem is for Beauty & Wellness businesses.
2. **Data Availability** – Whether suitable public or accessible datasets are available.
3. **Data Science Scope** – Whether meaningful Data Science, Machine Learning, or NLP techniques can be applied.
4. **Feasibility** – Whether the problem can realistically be explored within the internship.
5. **Business Insights** – Whether the analysis can produce actionable insights.
6. **Future Scope** – Whether the problem can be extended into a larger Data Science project.

## 5. Initial Observation

Customer Sentiment Analysis appears to be a strong candidate because beauty product review datasets can provide large amounts of customer-generated text and rating information.

Public datasets such as Amazon Beauty reviews and Sephora beauty product review datasets contain information that can support customer feedback analysis.

Such data can potentially be used to study customer satisfaction, product preferences, recurring complaints, and product-related opinions.

However, the final business problem will be selected after further research into dataset availability, data quality, business relevance, and the scope of the internship.

---

## 6. Preliminary Preferred Problem

Based on the initial comparison, the current preferred problem is:

**Customer Sentiment Analysis of Beauty Product Reviews**

The broader business objective would be to use customer feedback data to understand customer satisfaction, identify recurring complaints and positive product attributes, and generate insights that can support product improvement and marketing decisions.

The problem will be formally finalized after completing additional research.

## 7. Conclusion

The Beauty & Wellness industry provides several opportunities for Data Science applications. Customer sentiment analysis, customer segmentation, product recommendation, trend prediction, and demand forecasting are promising areas.

At this stage, Customer Sentiment Analysis is considered the leading candidate because it combines strong business relevance, accessible review data, NLP applications, and opportunities for generating actionable customer insights.

Further research will be conducted before finalizing the business problem.
